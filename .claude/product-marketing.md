# Product marketing context — Tskvili (წყვილი) / Cute Couple Gifts

> Read by the marketing-skills pack before any copy/strategy work. Fill the TODOs.

## Business
- **What**: Instagram-first dropshipping shop for romantic / couple gifts in Georgia.
  Products are sourced on 1688 / Taobao (via CSSBuy), curated by AI (Gemini vision) in the
  DropOS backoffice, and posted to Instagram. Orders come through Instagram DMs/comments;
  the owner fulfils manually.
- **Brand names**: Tskvili (წყვილი = "couple") — also referred to as Cute Couple Gifts.
- **Market**: Georgia (Tbilisi first). Language of all customer-facing copy: **Georgian**
  (captions, replies, hashtags mixed Georgian + English).
- **Price range**: ₾40–₾119 per item (GEL). Typical margin 60–85% over landed cost.
- **Channels**: Instagram posts (single image / carousel / collage), DMs for orders.
  TODO: TikTok? Stories? website/checkout?

## Audience
- Gen-Z couples, ages 16–26, buying a gift for their partner (one person buys for the other).
- Triggers: anniversaries, Valentine's, birthdays, "thinking of you", long-distance.
- Mindset: "would a 20-year-old see this on TikTok and immediately send it to her boyfriend
  saying 'omg we need this'?" — impulse, cute, emotional, screenshot-worthy.
- Aesthetics that work: dark romance, minimalist silver, soft pink / coquette, Y2K, kawaii.

## Products that sell (examples)
matching jewelry sets (necklaces, bracelets, rings), projection "I love you in 100 languages"
necklaces, long-distance touch lamps / bracelets, open-when letter kits, reasons-I-love-you jars,
star projectors / galaxy lamps, romantic neon signs, coquette accessories (bows, pearls, hearts),
cute plushies with a couple angle.

Hard no: generic gift boxes, wedding/engagement rings, his/hers mugs & hoodies (oversaturated),
children's toys, kitchen/office/industrial, anything needing explanation to feel romantic.

## Voice
- Warm, playful, slightly teasing; speaks like a friend, not a store. Emoji-friendly.
- Short Georgian sentences; the hook first, product second, how-to-order last.
- CTA pattern: "მომწერე 'მინდა' და გაგიგზავნი ფასს 💌" / "DM to order" — orders happen in DMs.
